# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary: a Yahoo fantasy basketball manager mid-draft.** They have the Yahoo draft room in one window and this tool in another, often at half-screen width. They have seconds per nomination, and they need one number: how high to bid on this player, right now, in this league.

**Secondary: a hiring manager for PM roles,** landing from Gurnek Khaira's portfolio. They judge whether the product is scoped well and whether its numbers can be trusted.

## Product Purpose

Turns the commissioner's budget and the room's spending into a live, defensible price for every player in a salary cap draft. Success: the manager never has to guess whether a bid is too high, and knows which categories their roster still needs.

## Brand

Working title "Cap Drafter". The tone of a draft-room tool: dense, calm and numeric. Visual world: an arena scoreboard, with a matte black fascia, amber lamp figures for money and coral red only for refused or over-max picks (chosen 2026-10-16). Full system in DESIGN.md. Category cells use blue for helps and red for hurts, and always show the number too, so colour is never the only signal.

## Proof

- Valuation method in `docs/adr/0002-valuation-method.md`, with tests asserting that values spend exactly the league's money.
