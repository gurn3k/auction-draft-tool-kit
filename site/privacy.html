Privacy

Updated November 13, 2026. Covers the website and the Chrome extension, and how Yahoo sync will work once it arrives.

The short version

Cap Drafter doesn't ask who you are and doesn't collect anything about you. Your league settings and draft picks stay in your own browser.

What stays on your device

League settings, team names, picks, the values you set yourself and any projections file you upload are saved in your browser's local storage, so a refresh doesn't lose your draft. They never leave your device. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them all. Reset draft clears the picks and keeps the rest.

The website

The website counts visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and doesn't identify you. It records pages viewed and rough details such as country and device type, in aggregate.

Coming later: Yahoo sync

Yahoo sync isn't live yet. It's built, and waits on Yahoo approving Cap Drafter's access to its Fantasy API. This page will be updated before it goes live. Here's how it's designed:

  • It's optional. Without it, Cap Drafter works exactly as it does today.
  • You sign in on Yahoo's own page and allow read-only access to your fantasy sports data. Cap Drafter never sees your Yahoo password, and can't change anything in your Yahoo account.
  • It reads only what the draft needs: your NBA leagues, one league's settings and teams, and its draft results while the draft runs.
  • Your Yahoo sign-in is kept in one encrypted cookie that page scripts can't read. There's no database, so nothing from Yahoo is stored on a server.
  • Signing out deletes that cookie. You can also remove Cap Drafter's access in your Yahoo account settings at any time.

The Chrome extension

The extension opens the same app in Chrome's side panel. It contains no analytics and sends nothing anywhere. It asks for one permission, the side panel, and doesn't read or change any website you visit, including your draft room.

Feedback

Feedback goes through a GitHub issue, which is public and covered by GitHub's privacy statement. Don't include personal details you don't want public.
