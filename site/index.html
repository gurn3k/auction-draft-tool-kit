Value is the pre-draft price. Live re-prices the players left using the money and spots left. Bid to is Live, adjusted up to ±20% for your roster’s category needs (switch this off in League setup), and capped at what you can afford. Category cells are z-scores against the players who get drafted: blue helps, red hurts. Type a nominated player in the search box, or click his row, to put him on the block.
